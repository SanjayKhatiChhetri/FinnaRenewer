# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with Oulu Finna library cards who borrow physical books and forget due dates — students, families, heavy readers. They visit briefly (check loans, link a card, glance at history) between other tasks, often on a phone. The job: never pay a late fee, with zero ongoing effort.

## Product Purpose

Finna Renewer auto-renews library loans before they're due via a weekly cron, and shows loans/holds/fines at a glance. Success: a user links a card once, then never thinks about due dates again — the dashboard exists to confirm "you're safe," not to demand attention.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bookish, friendly. A warm library companion, not a sterile admin tool. Copy talks like a helpful librarian; illustrations and micro-delights are welcome at earned moments (renewal success, empty shelves, first-run). Never noisy — the playfulness seasons a calm, trustworthy base.

## Anti-references

- Sterile SaaS admin dashboards (generic sidebar + stat-card grids with no voice).
- Gamified reading apps (streaks, badges, XP) — renewing books is not a game.
- Brutalist/terminal aesthetics — wrong audience, wrong warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Confirm safety at a glance.** The primary screen answers "am I going to get a late fee?" in one second — status color and due-date hierarchy before anything else.
2. **Delight at earned moments only.** Renewal success, empty shelves, first link — those moments get personality. Routine rows stay quiet.
3. **Bookish, not childish.** Literary touches (book covers, shelf metaphors, librarian voice) over cartoon mascots.
4. **The tool disappears.** Auto-renew is the product; the UI's job is trust — accurate data, visible sync times, honest error states.
5. **One vocabulary everywhere.** Same loan-card anatomy, same badge meanings, same button shapes on every screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body-text contrast, visible focus indicators, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation, 44px touch targets on mobile, semantic HTML with ARIA where needed.
